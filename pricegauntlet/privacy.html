Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy describes how PriceGauntlet Uploader (“the app”), operated by the owner of the PriceGauntlet YouTube channel, handles information.

Who uses the app

The app is a private tool used only by the channel owner to manage their own YouTube channel. It has no other users.

What data the app accesses

The app uses YouTube API Services with these permissions, only for the owner's own channel:

  • youtube.upload: to upload the owner's videos to the channel;
  • youtube.force-ssl: to post the channel's own comments under its own videos and to add videos to the channel's playlists;
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read the channel's own aggregate statistics (such as views and average percentage watched per video), used only to decide which kinds of quizzes to make more of.

It does not collect or store personal information about viewers, and it does not read viewers' data beyond the channel's aggregate statistics.

How data is stored

The OAuth token issued by Google is stored only on the channel owner's own computer and as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository, where the owner's automated workflow uses it to upload videos. No data is sent to any server other than Google's. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube policies

By using the app you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
