PriceGauntlet

Money quizzes on YouTube, built on real data.

PriceGauntlet publishes short quiz videos: guess what everyday things cost today compared with years ago, what an old dollar is worth now, and where in the world a Big Mac costs the most. Every number comes from official sources such as the US Bureau of Labor Statistics and The Economist's Big Mac index.

PriceGauntlet Uploader

PriceGauntlet Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the PriceGauntlet YouTube channel. It uploads videos that the channel owner has created to their own channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public.
