Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This policy describes how Geo Shorts Uploader (“the app”), operated by the owner of the QuizRush Global YouTube channel, handles information.

Who uses the app

The app is a private tool used only by the channel owner to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channel. It has no other users.

What data the app accesses

The app uses YouTube API Services and requests a single permission: youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos to the signed-in user’s channel. It does not read, collect or store viewer data, comments, analytics, contacts or any other personal information.

How data is stored

The OAuth access token issued by Google is stored only on the channel owner’s own computer and is used solely to upload videos. No data is sent to any server other than Google’s. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the locally stored token also removes the app’s access.

Google and YouTube policies

By using the app you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
